# Acme Co — Employee Handbook

## Welcome

Welcome to Acme Co. We build tools that help small businesses manage their
invoices and payments online. Our mission is to make invoicing simple and
reliable for freelancers and small teams.

## Refund policy

Customers can request a refund within 14 days of their purchase, provided the
service has not been fully delivered. To request a refund, contact support at
support@acme.example.com with your invoice number. Refunds are processed within
5 business days and are returned using the original payment method.

## Remote work policy

Acme Co is a fully remote company. Employees can work from anywhere, provided
they overlap with the team between 13:00 and 16:00 UTC for meetings. Company
laptops are shipped to employees, and a monthly internet allowance is paid with
the salary.

## Vacation days

Each employee receives 25 paid vacation days per year, plus the public holidays
of their country of residence. Vacation requests are approved by the team lead
and should be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

## Security guidelines

- Use a password manager and enable two-factor authentication on all accounts.
- Never share credentials over email or chat.
- Report suspicious emails to security@acme.example.com immediately.
